    The Jaydaar Way

    Batik is patience made visible — wax drawn by hand, colour laid down layer by layer, and a fine crackle that makes every piece one of a kind. We dye in bold, joyful colour , cut for ease and real life, and finish every saree and set to be worn for years . A style that is timeless.

    The Atelier

    Drawn in wax.
    Made to last.

    Jaydaar began with a love for Sri Lankan batik — the slow, careful craft of drawing in hot wax, dyeing, cracking and dyeing again until a pattern appears that no machine could repeat. We bring that craft into sarees, sets and dresses you'll reach for again and again: bold in colour, easy to wear, and made to stay in your wardrobe for years.

    • 01
      Hand-waxed batik

      Every motif is drawn by hand in wax, so no two pieces are ever quite alike.

    • 02
      Breathable cotton

      Soft cottons that stay cool through the heat of the day and drape beautifully.

    • 03
      Considered fit

      Free-size sarees and sarongs, and sets and dresses in sizes XS to XL.
